Inside Quantum Technology (IQT) featured QuSecure Co-Founder and CEO Rebecca Krauthamer on The Quantum Dragon Podcast, where she discussed the growing divide between organizations preparing early for post-quantum cryptography and those delaying action. The conversation also covered U.S. military cryptographic standards, AWS Qualified Software Certification, and why organizations should begin planning for quantum security today.
